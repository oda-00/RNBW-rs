RNBW VarArena per-allocation generation: final A/B
Date: 2026-11-09

Before: `store-current-final-2026-11-09.txt`; after: `store-after-arena-generation-final-2026-11-09.txt`. Same host (AVX2), optimized Criterion build, 1M-key datasets, 10 samples, 100 ms warmup, 150 ms target with Criterion extending slow groups. Runs are sequential and unpaired; differences are directional, especially changes below a few percent.
The correctness fix stores a per-allocation generation and live bit in the existing 8-byte VAR header; the handle remains `{generation:20,page:20,offset:24}`. It prevents stale-handle reuse without increasing allocated record bytes. The change also removes the redundant 8-byte generation field from each allocated page.

Compared 95 of 95 timing groups. >3%: 16 faster, 48 slower. Median of per-group after/before ratios: +3.24%.
