Conclusion: decode and encode+decode medians moved both directions across the corpus, and encode controls also varied despite identical code. This A/B did not establish a repeatable speedup. The implementation change and its temporary unit test were removed; no codec behavior or serialized bytes changed. The proposed path would have removed the one-element outer `Vec<Vec<u8>>` collection on compressed single-lane decode, but its benefit was below the noise floor of whole-workload measurements.
