Every row keeps its complete timed operation and reports the timed center divided by the operation count shown. The original Criterion interval for each whole operation is in `store-trnmap-current-summary-2026-11-09.txt`. Normalized values are throughput amortization, not isolated latency unless the denominator is one call.

Denominators come from the benchmark loops in `benches/store.rs`: builds/decode use the dataset key count; membership uses the 1,024-query batch; insert/remove/drain time one named operation with reset/setup outside the timer; promotion divides by the 65,536 chunks visited by its declared throughput; no-op demotion divides the 256 calls in the timed batch; the dense insert/remove pair divides by its two mutations.

Only center estimates are normalized here. Use the source summary for the full confidence interval; dividing interval endpoints by the same denominator gives the matching normalized interval.
