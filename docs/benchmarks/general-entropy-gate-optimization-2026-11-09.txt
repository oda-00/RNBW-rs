RNBW high-entropy raw-fallback gate and single-lane raw serializer
Date: 2026-11-09
Change: broadened the sampled incompressibility gate (distinct bytes >=240, max sampled byte count <=1/16 of the sample, duplicate 3-grams <=32) and directly serialize a single raw lane. The serialized format is unchanged; raw payload is copied once into the final image, with only the header and alignment padding zero-filled.

Why: the 89,883-byte compressed-PNG corpus failed the old sampler (distinct=256, max frequency=81 versus limit 32, duplicate grams=25 versus limit 8), ran the full LZ+entropy path, then chose raw fallback. The broadened gate sends it directly to the same raw image.

Method: before and after used Criterion release profile, AVX2, 10 samples, 100 ms warmup, 150 ms requested measurement across 16 fixed general workloads. Corpus construction and comparator encoding are outside timed loops; roundtrip assertions run before timing. These are independent runs, not paired. A focused confirmation used 30 samples, 200 ms warmup and 500 ms measurement for the four affected serial/parallel encode cases.

The all-corpus unpaired geomeans were 1.419x reuse-parallel encode, 1.476x reuse-serial encode, and 1.447x one-shot encode. Non-overlapping intervals were mixed outside the two targeted datasets (parallel 4 improved/5 regressed/7 overlapped; serial 8/3/5; one-shot 2/5/9), so no general-corpus speedup is claimed beyond the two confirmed raw-fallback inputs. Decode source is unchanged; its independent-run medians were mixed and are not claimed as a codec decode change.

Correctness: full `cargo test --offline --locked` passed (46 library tests, 3 binary tests, 1 codec mutation-fuzz test, 3 TRNMAP mutation-fuzz tests). After the full run, the two raw-path tests were rerun and passed with additional byte-for-byte checks: compressed PNG fast output equals both old raw serialization and the prior full-LZ-then-raw-fallback image; random binary equals the prior raw serializer.
