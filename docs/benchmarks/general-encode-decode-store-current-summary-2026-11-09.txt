RNBW current encode/decode and store benchmark results
Date: 2026-11-09

## Method
- Windows host, AVX2 dispatch selected.
- General files: `cargo bench --offline --locked --bench files -- 'file-(encode|decode)/' --sample-size 10 --warm-up-time 0.05 --measurement-time 0.05 --noplot --quiet`.
- 16 byte corpora; 383 Criterion measurements. The harness asserts successful serial and parallel RNBW roundtrips and comparator roundtrips. Eight external comparisons: LZ4, Snappy, Zstd fast-3 / level-1, Brotli quality 0 / 5, and zlib fast / default.
- Store: same Criterion settings, five 1M-key-shaped data distributions and p32/p64 layouts. Build includes the RNBW L2 store, L2+L3 full-range promoted representation, and RNBW query image; decode times include L2 and promoted stores.
- Ten Criterion samples and 50 ms warm-up/measurement target are a quick current sweep. Criterion adapts iterations to operation cost and records confidence intervals in raw logs. These are absolute current timings, not a paired codec-change A/B. Compare medians cautiously; no hardware counters were collected.

| clustered | 0.971 | 0.908 |

Only uniform p32 has a captured pre-change A/B baseline: 454.070 ms (Criterion interval `446.48 ms 454.07 ms 462.78 ms`) vs 57.161 ms candidate (interval `56.425 ms 57.161 ms 57.854 ms`). That is 87.4% lower median time (7.94x faster). The candidate decodes each 16-chunk group once per refresh. Other workload rows are candidate-only and are not claimed as speedups.

## TRNMAP and codec boundary

The file harness builds and correctness-checks one TRNMAP classification index over raw u32 words for each input before benchmarking the byte codec. It separately times serial/reused/parallel RNBW encode and RNBW decode. The LZ encoder still uses a fixed two-way, direct-address hash-head table to map 3-byte hashes to two recent source positions; `TrnMap` is not called by the LZ match search or decoder. Thus these encode/decode figures do not prove a TRNMAP-backed LZ codec speedup.
